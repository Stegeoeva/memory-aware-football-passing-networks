The original roadmap's first four scientific stages are implemented. The community stage is not. A central notebook presents the completed work after local reproduction. Public notebook outputs are cleared; executed local copies and detailed outputs are not redistributed. No remote publication is performed during preparation.

Follow-up priorities are memory-state communities, team/competition-aware external validation, better outcome baselines, failure-inclusive sequence formation and tracking-derived decision context. The [lineup policy](LINEUP_POLICY.md) retains short intervals for audit and requires five minutes/20 passes for main figures.

The original protocol was internal planning, not an external preregistration. The three case studies had already been inspected and are not an untouched test set.
